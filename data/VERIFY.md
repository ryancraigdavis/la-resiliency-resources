# For the co-chairs: please check before launch

These items came up while moving the three Google Docs (dated June 30, 2026) into the site.

Please skim `resources.yaml`, or the Google Sheet once it's set up.

## Please confirm (high stakes)

- [ ] **Tenant Power Toolkit, the "5 days to respond" tip.** California law AB 2347 (effective
      January 2025) appears to have changed the deadline to respond to an eviction lawsuit to
      10 court days. Please confirm with Stay Housed L.A. or the Toolkit before we publish a
      deadline.
- [ ] **Full Scope Medi-Cal, "Ages 50+".** Medi-Cal eligibility for undocumented adults has
      changed several times since 2024, including a new enrollment freeze and benefit changes
      in 2026. Please confirm who can enroll right now, what is covered, and whether the
      "will NOT affect your immigration status" statement is still accurate.
- [ ] **CalFresh income limits** change every October 1. The next change is October 1, 2026.
- [ ] **Hotline numbers.** LARRN and CHIRLA both list (888) 624-4752. Please confirm it is
      still the rapid response number.

## Language help (the most important gap)

The site will show "Help in [your language]" only when we know an organization actually serves
that language. The docs list languages only for Asian Americans Advancing Justice. We could not
find anything marked for **Spanish, Farsi, or Turkish** speakers.

- [ ] For each organization, which languages can staff or hotlines help in? (Sheet: check the
      language boxes.)
- [ ] Do you know organizations that serve **Farsi** or **Turkish** speakers? Please add them.
      Until then, the site tells people to call 211 and ask for an interpreter.
