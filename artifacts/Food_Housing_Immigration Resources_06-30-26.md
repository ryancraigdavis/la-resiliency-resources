**Welfare & Self-Reliance Resources**

**FINDING RESOURCES**  
**211 LA**  
[211la.org  |  Call or text 211 (24/7, multilingual)](https://211la.org)  
LA County’s free helpline connecting residents to housing, food, health, emergency financial aid, and social services. Live Community Resource Advisors assess your situation and make referrals. Available in multiple languages around the clock.  
*Use 211 as your first call when you don’t know where to start.*

**FindHelp.org**  
[findhelp.org](https://www.findhelp.org)  
Nationwide database of local social services. Search by zip code for food, housing, immigration help, childcare, healthcare, and more. Free, confidential, and available in multiple languages.

**City of LA Residents Portal**  
[lacity.gov/residents](https://lacity.gov/residents)  
Central hub for all City of Los Angeles services — housing, food, emergency support, immigration, and more.

**HOUSING**  
**Housing Counseling — Neighborhood Housing Services of LA County**  
[nhslacounty.org  |  (888) 895-2647](https://www.nhslacounty.org)  
Free rental and housing counseling for LA County residents.

**Los Angeles Housing Department (LAHD)**  
[housing.lacity.gov  |  (866) 557-7368](https://housing.lacity.gov)  
City department overseeing tenant rights enforcement, rent stabilization, affordable housing programs, and first-time homebuyer assistance. Call to speak with a live person about housing options or to file a landlord complaint.

**Affordable Rental Housing Search — City of LA**  
[housing.lacity.gov/housing/affordable-housing](https://housing.lacity.gov/housing/affordable-housing)  
Searchable database of affordable rental housing throughout the City of Los Angeles.

**Eviction Defense — Stay Housed L.A.**  
[stayhousedla.org](https://www.stayhousedla.org)  
County/City partnership providing legal resources to help residents stay in their homes. Serves all of LA County.

**Eviction Defense — Tenant Power Toolkit**  
[tenantpowertoolkit.org](https://www.tenantpowertoolkit.org)  
Free tool that helps tenants file a legal response when served with eviction papers. Critical: tenants have only 5 days to respond after receiving court papers or risk automatic eviction.

**FOOD**  
**Food Bank & Pantry Locator — LA Regional Food Bank**  
[lafoodbank.org/find-food/pantry-locator](https://www.lafoodbank.org/find-food/pantry-locator/)  
Search by zip code or address to find nearby food pantries across LA County. Shows hours, contact info, and additional services at each location.

**Food Finder — 211 LA**  
[211la.org/food-resources](https://211la.org/food-resources)  
Online mapping tool to find food pantries, meal programs, and emergency food assistance near you.

**FamilySource Centers — Weekly Food Distribution**  
[LA4Families.org](https://www.la4families.org)  
The City of LA operates 19 FamilySource Centers offering free weekly distributions of fresh produce, pantry staples, and household groceries. No appointment needed. Open to all City of LA residents regardless of immigration status. Find your nearest location by zip code at LA4Families.org.

**Cal-Fresh (SNAP / EBT / Food Stamps)**  
[benefitscal.com  |  (877) 847-3663](https://www.benefitscal.com)  
Monthly food assistance loaded onto an EBT card. Must be a U.S. citizen or Qualified Legal Resident.  
Income limits: 1 person — $2,878/mo  |  2 people — $3,894/mo  |  3 people — $4,910/mo  
Adults 18–50 without children must work 20 hrs/week. Special consideration for seniors and students.

**CFAP — California Food Assistance Program**  
*For immigrants who don’t qualify for CalFresh due to immigration status.*  
[benefitscal.com  |  (877) 847-3663  |  or your local DPSS office](https://www.benefitscal.com)  
State-funded. Same monthly benefit as CalFresh. If you lose CalFresh due to an immigration status change, your county automatically evaluates you for CFAP — no separate application needed.

**WIC — Women, Infants & Children**  
[phfewic.org](https://www.phfewic.org)  
For pregnant/breastfeeding women, women who recently lost a child, and children under 5 — all low income. Auto-qualifies if already on Cal-Fresh. Provides EBT card for healthy foods plus nutrition support.

**Market Match**  
Bonus farmers’ market vouchers for Cal-Fresh and WIC recipients.  
Cal-Fresh: up to $15 in extra vouchers  |  WIC: $10 at East Hollywood Certified Farmers’ Market

**East Hollywood Certified Farmers’ Market**  
*Hollywood & Western  |  Mondays & Thursdays, 3:30–7:30pm*  
California-grown produce. Accepts Cal-Fresh and WIC. Serves 20,000+ customers annually.

**Emergency Food Resources — City of LA**  
[emergency.lacity.gov/FoodAssistance](https://emergency.lacity.gov/FoodAssistance)  
Searchable map of food distribution sites, pantries, and hot meal programs across Los Angeles.

**Meals for Seniors — LA Regional Food Bank / CSFP**  
*(800) 510-2020*  
The Commodity Supplemental Food Program (CSFP) serves 27,800+ seniors monthly with free food packages. Call to find the nearest distribution site and enroll.

**LAUSD Free Meals for Children**  
All LAUSD students receive free breakfast and lunch Monday–Friday. Weekend meal kits also available at participating schools.

**Bishop’s Storehouse — The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints**  
The Bishop’s Storehouse provides food and household essentials to members in need at no cost — functioning similarly to a grocery store. Access requires a Bishop’s order. After you have explored the community resources listed above and still have unmet needs, you are encouraged to meet with your Bishop. Together you will assess your situation, develop a plan toward self-reliance, and your Bishop can issue a storehouse order as appropriate.

**IMMIGRATION**  
**CAPI — Cash Assistance Program for Immigrants**  
[dpss.lacounty.gov](https://dpss.lacounty.gov)  
For immigrants who qualify for SSI but are ineligible due to immigration status. Same monthly benefit as SSI. Must show proof of SSI application.

**Full Scope Medi-Cal — Ages 50+**  
Free comprehensive health insurance regardless of immigration status.  
Covers: doctor visits  |  dental  |  vision  |  prescriptions  |  in-home care  |  transportation  
Getting Medi-Cal will NOT affect your immigration status.  
*Narrow exception: if used to pay for long-term nursing home care — consult an immigration attorney.*

**Know Your Rights**  
**ACLU Know Your Rights**  
[aclu.org/know-your-rights](https://www.aclu.org/know-your-rights)  
A plain-language guide to your legal rights during immigration enforcement encounters — what you must provide, what you can refuse, and what to do if detained. Available in multiple languages.

**Know Your Rights — City of Los Angeles**  
[mayor.lacity.gov/KYR](https://mayor.lacity.gov/KYR)  
The Mayor’s Office Know Your Rights guide for immigrants in LA — covers what to do during immigration enforcement, City services available regardless of status, and where to find legal help. Available in multiple languages.

**Know Your Rights — California Attorney General**  
[oag.ca.gov/immigrant](https://oag.ca.gov/immigrant)  
State-level immigrant protections, rights during enforcement encounters, workplace protections, and family preparedness resources. Available in multiple languages.

**If Someone Is Detained — Rapid Response**  
**LARRN — Los Angeles Rapid Response Network**  
*(888) 624-4752*  
A coalition of 60+ community, labor, and faith-based organizations that responds to immigration enforcement actions across LA. Call to report ICE activity, request legal observation, or get an emergency legal intake. Can deploy attorneys for deportation defense.

**ACLU SoCal Rapid Response Networks**  
[aclusocal.org/california-rapid-response-networks](https://www.aclusocal.org/california-rapid-response-networks/)  
Network of rapid response teams across Southern California. Contact to report immigration enforcement activity or if a loved one has been detained.

**CHIRLA Rapid Response & Legal Assistance**  
[chirla.org](https://www.chirla.org)  
Rapid Response Hotline: (888) 624-4752  
Immigrant Assistance Referral Line: (213) 201-3797  |  (213) 201-8773  
Provides Know Your Rights cards, guidance on locating a detained family member, document checklists, and legal assistance referrals.
