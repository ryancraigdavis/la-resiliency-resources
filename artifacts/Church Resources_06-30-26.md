**Perpetual Education Fund**  
[https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/education/perpetual-education-fund?lang=eng](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/education/perpetual-education-fund?lang=eng)  
Members of the Church all over the world sacrifice to donate money to the Perpetual Education Fund. The Perpetual Education Fund (PEF) helps members of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints by providing financial aid for education that will lead to better employment opportunities and increased self-reliance. The PEF program was announced by President Gordon B. Hinckley in 2001 and has helped more than 110,000 individuals since.

When he announced the creation of the Perpetual Education Fund, President Hinckley taught that participants “will repay their loans to make it possible for others to be blessed as they have been blessed” (“The Perpetual Education Fund,” Ensign, May 2001, 52). The Perpetual Education Fund is only “perpetual” if participants give back what they have received. By paying back a PEF loan, we prove our integrity and allow others to partake of the blessings of education and self-reliance. This money is sacred, and it should be used only to help fund your education to lead to better work and self-reliance.

**SELF-RELIANCE**  
Heavenly Father loves each of His children and has provided ways to increase self-reliance and find the strength to overcome difficulties.  
[https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/self-reliance?lang=eng](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/self-reliance?lang=eng)

By building self-reliance, individuals can achieve greater independence, self-respect, and spiritual growth, which can prepare the way for a more fulfilling life. The Lord has said, “It is my purpose to provide for my saints . . . but it must needs be done in mine own way” ([Doctrine and Covenants 104:15, 16](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/study/scriptures/dc-testament/dc/104.15,16?lang=eng&_r=1#14)). 

This is a promise that Heavenly Father will help take care of His children as they follow Him. Self-reliance does not mean that we can accomplish or obtain anything we want. If we are self-reliant, we believe that through the power of Christ, and through individual effort, we can work for the spiritual and practical needs of life. 

Individuals can become more self-reliant. The self-reliance initiative is a tool to help.

**SELF-RELIANCE COURSES**

These are practical courses, which provide temporal and spiritual benefits.  
[https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/self-reliance/courses?lang=eng](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/life/self-reliance/courses?lang=eng)

Groups are focused on one of several areas: 

1. **Education for Better Work**  
   Education can give you the skills, knowledge, and confidence to unlock better opportunities, improve your life, and lift those around you.  
     
2. **Find a Better Job**  
   In today’s competitive job market, having the right skills can make all the difference.  
   Participants learn to identify opportunities, network, present themselves professionally, and prepare for interviews.  
     
3. **English*Connect***   
   Learn English. Expand your opportunities.  
   Develop your English skills in an environment of faith, fellowship, and growth. EnglishConnect has brought together more than 100,000 learners in over 100 countries.  
     
4. **Finding Strength in the Lord: Emotional Resilience**  
   Life’s challenges can be overwhelming, but you don’t have to face them alone.  
   Learn how to cultivate positive thinking, strengthen relationships, and improve your mental well-being. Most importantly, discover how to draw strength and support from God to face challenges with confidence.  
     
5. **Life Skills**  
   [https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/self-reliance/course-materials/life-skills?lang=eng](https://www.churchofjesuschrist.org/self-reliance/course-materials/life-skills?lang=eng)  
   For older youth and young single adults.  
   *Life Skills for Self-Reliance* contains the most applicable content from three self-reliance manuals (*Personal Finances,* *Find a Better Job,* and *Education for Better Work*).   
   This group helps participants research career and education opportunities, find and obtain employment, and learn how to manage their money. It will prepare them for missions, future education, military service, and work after high school.  
     
6. **Personal Finances**  
   Effectively managing your resources prepares you for life’s ups and downs, builds confidence, and strengthens your self-reliance.  
     
7. **Starting and Growing My Business**  
   Whether you’re just starting out or looking to expand, this self-reliance course offers the tools, resources, and support you need to succeed.
